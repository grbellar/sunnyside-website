Round eight: more Clear morning and Butter

  • Palette sheetAll seven side by side with swatches, contrast ratios, the marks recolored and Coolors links. Start here.

  • Morning, one blueClear morning with the bright blue removed. Links and buttons are navy ink, so the sky is the only blue and the sun the only bright color.
  • Sky and butterClear morning and Butter combined: sky stage, blue links, and yellow buttons with navy text.

Round seven: G2a in seven palettes

  • Palette sheetAll seven side by side with swatches, contrast ratios, the three marks recolored and a link to open each in Coolors. Start here.
  • Clear morningThe current direction, tuned: a softer sky, a deeper blue that reads less like a default link, and warm paper instead of pure white.
  • PrairieWheat-colored stage with a deep Kansas-sky blue for links and buttons. Warm and cool in balance.
  • Golden hourApricot stage, terracotta accent, warm near-black ink. Picks up the orange cap in the photo.
  • DuskLavender stage with an indigo accent. Violet sits opposite yellow on the wheel, so the sun stands out most here.
  • Sky and clayCool blue-gray stage with a clay accent. Complementary blue and orange, with the orange kept for actions.
  • LakeSage stage and a deep green accent, taken from the lake behind you in the photo.
  • ButterThe loudest option: a butter-yellow stage, near-black ink for text and buttons, no second color.

  • G3b. Blue sideThe dark half takes the button blue. The hero link list is set in large Instrument Serif italic. Instrument Serif, Instrument Sans, Homemade Apple.
  • G3c. CrescentLight falling on a sphere, lit from the upper left, which also nods to the old sun and moon site. Instrument Serif over Hanken Grotesk. Instrument Serif, Hanken Grotesk, Homemade Apple.
