Hi,

I’m Grant. I work with founders and small business owners who have built a first version of their idea themselves, with an AI tool, a no-code builder, or a lot of late nights, and have hit the wall.

You probably know the signs. It demos well, but it breaks when real people use it. Every fix creates a new problem. Payments, logins, or customer data feel risky to touch. You’re spending more time keeping it alive than growing the business.

That’s normal, and it’s the point where most people go looking for help. An agency will take months and put a project manager between you and the code. A freelancer from a marketplace is quick to hire, but you can’t tell if they’re any good until it’s too late.

With me, you work directly with the person doing the work. I’ll go through what you have, tell you honestly what’s worth keeping, and give you a plan and a price before we start. Then I rebuild what needs rebuilding, launch it, and stay on to keep it running as you grow.

You own everything, including the code, the accounts and the domain. I write things down and explain decisions in plain English, so you’re never stuck depending on me.

If that sounds like where you are, send me a link to what you’ve built. I read and answer every message.
