# Site copy drafts

Audience: non-technical founders and small business owners with an early idea or a rough prototype, who have the budget to build it properly.
Up against: agencies that move too slowly, and marketplace freelancers of unknown quality.
Mobile apps are dropped from the headline.

Two versions below. A leads with the prototype someone already made. B leads with the idea, and the prototype is one of the ways in.

Headline for both: **Your idea, built and kept running by one person you can call.**

---

## A. Prototype first

### Hero

**Your idea, built and kept running by one person you can call.**

Subhead options:
1. You built a prototype that proves the idea. I'm Grant Bellar, a software engineer in Wichita, Kansas, and I turn it into a product people can sign up for, pay for, and rely on.
2. Made something with an AI tool or a no-code builder, and now it's falling over? I'm Grant Bellar, and I turn rough prototypes into stable products you can launch.
3. I'm Grant Bellar, a software engineer in Wichita, Kansas. I take the prototype you built yourself and make it ready for real customers.

Button: **Show me what you've built**

### Letter

> Hi,
>
> I'm Grant. I work with founders and small business owners who have built a first version of their idea themselves, with an AI tool, a no-code builder, or a lot of late nights, and have hit the wall.
>
> You probably know the signs. It demos well, but it breaks when real people use it. Every fix creates a new problem. Payments, logins, or customer data feel risky to touch. You're spending more time keeping it alive than growing the business.
>
> That's normal, and it's the point where most people go looking for help. An agency will take months and put a project manager between you and the code. A freelancer from a marketplace is quick to hire, but you can't tell if they're any good until it's too late.
>
> With me, you work directly with the person doing the work. I'll go through what you have, tell you honestly what's worth keeping, and give you a plan and a price before we start. Then I rebuild what needs rebuilding, launch it, and stay on to keep it running as you grow.
>
> You own everything, including the code, the accounts and the domain. I write things down and explain decisions in plain English, so you're never stuck depending on me.
>
> If that sounds like where you are, send me a link to what you've built. I read and answer every message.

### What I take on

- **Prototype to product.** Your prototype proves the idea. I make it handle real users, real data, and payments.
- **Rescue.** A previous developer left, or the project stalled halfway. I'll read what's there, tell you what's salvageable, and finish it.
- **After launch.** Fixes, new features, hosting and backups, handled every month so you don't need to hire.

### How it goes

1. **Look.** You send me what you have. I go through it and we talk.
2. **Plan.** A written plan and a fixed price before anything starts.
3. **Build.** You see working progress every week.
4. **Launch and look after.** It goes live, and I stay on as it grows.

### Contact

**Show me what you've built.**
A link, a screen recording, or a description is enough. I reply within a day.

Fields:
- Email
- Link to your prototype (optional)
- Where is it now? *Prototype only me uses / Testing with a few users / Live with paying customers*
- Budget *Under $10k / $10k to $25k / $25k to $50k / $50k+*
- What's going wrong, and what do you need it to do?
