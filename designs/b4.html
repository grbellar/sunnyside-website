Hi,

I’m Grant. I run Sunnyside, and I’m the person who will design, build, and ship your app.

Most studios sell you a team and then hand your project to whoever is free. Here you get me, from the first call to the App Store. That keeps things fast, and it means the person you explain your business to is the person writing the code.

If that sounds like what you need, send me a note. I read and answer every one.
