Privacy

This site is run by Sunnyside Solutions LLC in Wichita, Kansas. It’s a single page with a contact form. Here is everything it collects and what happens to it.

What we collect

Only what you type into the contact form: your email address, an optional link, the options you pick and your message.

How it’s used

To reply to you and, if we work together, to do the work. We don’t sell it, rent it or add you to a mailing list.

Who else handles it

  • Form messages are delivered by Formspree, which passes them to our inbox.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google sees your IP address when the page loads.

The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ad trackers.

Your choices

Ask us to see or delete anything you’ve sent and we’ll do it. Use the contact form and say what you need.
