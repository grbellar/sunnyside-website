I’m Grant. I work with founders and small business owners who have hit the wall with software.

For some, it’s a first version of their idea, built with an AI tool, a no-code builder, or a lot of late nights. It demos well, but it breaks when real people use it. For others, it’s the business itself: orders retyped from one system into another, or software that does most of what you need while the rest gets done by hand.

An agency will take months and put a project manager between you and the code. A freelancer from a marketplace is quick to hire, but you can’t tell if they’re any good until it’s too late.

With me, you work directly with the person doing the work. Before I suggest anything, I want to understand how your business runs and where it hurts. The first project is often small, like a report that builds itself or a checkout that stops failing. If it goes well, we take on the next thing.

You own everything, including the code, the accounts and the data. I explain decisions in plain English, so you’re never stuck depending on me.

Automation

Applicants who answer their own questions

Automation

Catching fake applicants

Rings of fake applicants reused phones, emails and resumes, and recruiters lost hours to them.

What I built
A risk score from simple, checkable signals that flags them before anyone gets on a call.
What changed
Of 7,571 applicants, 101 were flagged high risk, and none of the real hires were.
